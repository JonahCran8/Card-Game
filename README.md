This is a game prototype that has a player and an opponent facing each other.

Both player and opponent have 500 HP (Health Points) and the goal is to reduce the opponents HP to zero before your HP goes to 0.
This is done by playing cards, each turn the player has two seperate opportunities to play cards. You play one card at the same time as your opponent, then you play a second card at the same time.
Once both cards have been played, their effects are used. Effects can be direct damage, stats effects, bonuses and other things. 
Both players have an attack and defense stat which can be increased or decreased by played cards. In this prototype there are two elements, flame and earth which
have no functional difference at the moment other then colour and theme.
